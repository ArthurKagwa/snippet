Delete your Snippet account

This removes your account and every card you saved.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Snippet and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account and data.
  3. Confirm. You may be asked to re-confirm with Google.

Without the app

Email support@snippetverses.app from the address on your Google account, with the subject "Delete my Snippet account". We will delete your account and saved cards within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

Your sign-in record (Google account ID, name, email) and all saved cards in our database. Data kept only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
